# Flesh Made Fear - First Person

By [heuristickhorana](https://www.nexusmods.com/profile/heuristickhorana).

Plays the game through the character's eyes. The camera sits at head height and follows wherever your character's face points. The game's fixed camera angles are only used when you switch back.

- Toggle first/third person with **F** (rebindable)
- Look up/down with **PageUp**/**PageDown** (the game swallows the mouse's vertical axis, so no mouse-look is possible)
- Adjustable eye height, FOV and key via a simple `settings.txt`
- Works with the Steam (Windows & Proton) release

Requires [UE4SS](https://github.com/UE4SS-RE/RE-UE4SS/releases) (experimental build). Install/uninstall instructions in the Files tab. Does not modify any game files.

Heads-up: the game was built for fixed cameras, so in first person some things may look off, cutscenes play through your own eyes (you can miss framed shots), and blood loss isn't visible (it's shown on the character model, which you can't see).
